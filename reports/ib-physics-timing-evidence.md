# IB Physics pacing evidence

Reviewed 12 September 2026. Read-only research; no question eligibility, engine, configuration or published data changed. Only general assessment documents and pre-2026 paper covers/markscheme allocation pages were consulted. No 2026 question-paper content was used.

The recommended simple implementation is **Current IB practice pace**. Remember the learner's own HL/SL choice separately from each historical source paper's level. A past SL relativity option question does not establish that the learner studies SL. Keep the timer off by default and apply the existing extra-time preference when it is enabled.

| Current assessment | Official total time | Official total marks | Derived average pace |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| SL Paper 1A + 1B together | 90 minutes | 45 = 25 + 20 | 120 seconds per mark |
| HL Paper 1A + 1B together | 120 minutes | 60 = 40 + 20 | 120 seconds per mark |
| SL Paper 2 | 90 minutes | 50 | 108 seconds per mark |
| HL Paper 2 | 150 minutes | 90 | 100 seconds per mark |

The [official current subject brief](https://ibo.org/globalassets/new-structure/recognition/pdfs/dp_sciences_physics_subject-brief_jan_2022_e.pdf), assessment table on PDF page 4, confirms the durations. The [official first-2025 specimen papers](https://ibo.org/globalassets/new-structure/programmes/dp/pdfs/physics-hl-sl-specimen-papers-1a-2b-2-en.pdf) confirm marks and durations on PDF pages 4 (HL 1A), 46 (HL 2), 94 (SL 1A), 114 (SL 1B), and 126 (SL 2). Paper 1A and 1B share the stated time; the 120-second target is a proportional practice allocation, not a separately prescribed IB time allowance for either booklet or an individual question.

The [official historical SL/HL brief](https://www.ibo.org/globalassets/new-structure/programmes/dp/pdfs/slhl-brief-en.pdf), page 2, independently confirms the 2016-model durations; [IB's 2021/2022 assessment note](https://www.ibo.org/globalassets/new-structure/university-admission/pdfs/learning-teaching-and-assessment-2021-2022-en.pdf), printed page 41, confirms normal Paper 3 maxima and its removal in those adapted exam sessions.

For this viewer, use 120 seconds per mark for MCQs and current Paper 1B/data-analysis practice. For written content practice, including former Paper 3 option questions now taught in the main syllabus, use the learner's current HL/SL Paper 2 pace. Historical questions explicitly classified as DATA can be practised at the current Paper 1B pace; keep that an explicit practice convention rather than claiming it was their original exam allocation. Do not use the simplified Paper 1/Paper 2 filter bucket to infer source timing.

The part target is marks multiplied by the chosen seconds-per-mark rate and then by the extra-time factor. The A5 viewer starts with an explicit HL learner preference because A5 is HL content in the current syllabus; the learner can change and save that preference. This default is a site choice, never inferred from an old paper's printed level. Without known marks, retain stopwatch behavior rather than inventing a target. Time to read shared context and unequal difficulty make per-part targets approximate; no additional per-question calibration is claimed here.
