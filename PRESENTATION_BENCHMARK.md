The standard is: readable metadata and crop without clutter; 44 px controls;
an advisory before answering when required; verdict first after answering;
then a concise diagnosis, primary guided route, collapsed alternatives,
knowledge checks, reflection and rating. At 320 px the page must not widen;
filters are one toggle away and progress tables scroll inside a labelled,
keyboard-focusable region.

This is an opt-in shared-engine presentation contract. It changes layout and
pupil-facing chrome, not correctness, marking, authored analysis or other
consumers’ card composition.
